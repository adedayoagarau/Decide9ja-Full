## 2. THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE

### Nigeria's Political Information Crisis

**95 million registered voters lack basic political knowledge:**

1. **Representation Gap:** Only 12% of Nigerians can correctly name their Senator or House of Representatives member (NOI Polls, 2023). Citizens cannot hold leaders accountable when they don't know who represents them.

2. **Accountability Vacuum:** ₦2.4 trillion is allocated annually to constituency projects, yet most citizens have no way to verify if these projects exist or were completed. Corruption thrives in this information darkness.

3. **Promise Tracking Failure:** Politicians make campaign promises but there's no centralized system to track fulfillment. By election time, voters forget what was promised.

4. **Information Fragmentation:** Political data exists across INEC, BudgIT, newspapers, and government websites - but ordinary citizens lack the time or digital literacy to navigate these sources.

5. **Civic Disengagement:** Low information leads to voter apathy. Only 27% of registered voters participated in the 2023 presidential election - the lowest turnout in Nigeria's democratic history.

### Why This Matters

Without informed citizens, democracy becomes a ritual rather than a mechanism for accountability. Politicians know voters can't track them, creating a system where electoral promises are meaningless and public funds disappear without consequence.

## 3. OUR SOLUTION

### Decide9ja: The "Everything App" for Nigerian Politics

**A WhatsApp-first AI platform that puts political intelligence in every Nigerian's pocket.**

#### Core Features (Built & Functional):

| Feature | How It Works | User Benefit |
|---------|--------------|--------------|
| **Representative Finder** | User texts location (state/LGA) → Bot returns all 7 levels of representation (President → LGA Chairman) with profiles | Know exactly who represents you in 30 seconds |
| **Politician Profiles** | 4,789+ profiles with party, position, voting records, contact info | Research any politician before voting |
| **Promise Tracker** | Database of campaign promises linked to delivery status | Hold politicians to their word |
| **Budget Monitor** | 74,000+ BudgIT records on constituency projects, FAAC allocations | See where your tax money goes |
| **Issue Reporter** | Citizens report problems (bad roads, no water) → Auto-routed to relevant official | Direct line to government accountability |
| **Election Results** | 2007-2023 results for all elections at all levels | Historical context for voting decisions |
| **Political History** | 1,646 Wikipedia articles on Nigerian political history | Understand how we got here |
| **Multilingual Support** | English, Pidgin, Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo | Reach all Nigerians |

#### Technology Stack:
- **AI:** Claude (Anthropic) + GPT-4 for natural language understanding
- **Knowledge Graph:** 8,392 entities from Wikidata, Wikipedia, INEC, BudgIT
- **Channels:** WhatsApp (primary), Web App, Voice (for accessibility)
- **Backend:** FastAPI + PostgreSQL (production-ready)
