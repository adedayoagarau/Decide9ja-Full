## PROBLEM STATEMENT

### What problem does your business solve? (Short version)
```
Only 12% of Nigerians can name their Senator or House Representative. Without knowing who represents them, citizens cannot hold leaders accountable. Political information is scattered across INEC websites, government portals, and news archives that ordinary Nigerians cannot navigate. This information darkness allows corruption to thrive - trillions in constituency projects disappear while promises go untracked. Decide9ja solves this by putting comprehensive political intelligence in every Nigerian's pocket through WhatsApp.
```

### Extended Version
```
Nigeria has 95 million registered voters, but most cannot answer basic questions about their representatives. The problem isn't apathy - it's access. Political data exists across INEC, BudgIT, newspapers, and government websites, but citizens lack the time or digital literacy to find it. Meanwhile:

- ₦2.4 trillion is allocated annually to constituency projects with no citizen verification
- Campaign promises disappear after elections with no tracking mechanism
- 2023 saw the lowest voter turnout in Nigeria's democratic history (27%)

When voters don't know who represents them, democracy becomes a ritual rather than accountability. Decide9ja fixes this by aggregating 8,392 politician profiles, 74,000 budget records, and decades of history into one AI-powered WhatsApp bot that speaks Pidgin, Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, and English.
```

## SOLUTION DESCRIPTION

### What is your product/service? (Short version)
```
Decide9ja is a WhatsApp-first AI platform for political intelligence. Users text their location and instantly receive their 7 levels of representation (President to LGA Chairman) with profiles, voting records, and contact information. The platform also tracks politician promises, monitors constituency project spending (74,000+ BudgIT records), allows citizens to report local issues, and provides election history from 2007-2023. Built on FastAPI with Claude AI, it supports English, Pidgin, Hausa, Yoruba, and Igbo - reaching all Nigerians regardless of literacy level.
```

### Core Features List
```
1. Representative Finder: Text location → Get all 7 levels of government representatives
2. Politician Profiles: 4,789+ profiles with party, voting records, contact info
3. Promise Tracker: Campaign promises linked to delivery status
4. Budget Monitor: 74,000+ constituency project and FAAC allocation records
5. Issue Reporter: Citizens report problems → Auto-routed to relevant officials
6. Election Results: 2007-2023 results at all levels
7. Political History: 1,646 articles covering 1960-present
8. Multilingual: English, Pidgin, Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo
```

### What makes your business unique?
```
1. First-of-its-kind: No comprehensive Nigerian political AI exists - we're creating the category
2. Data Moat: 8,392 entities from Wikidata, 74,000 BudgIT records, 1,646 historical articles - years of aggregation that competitors cannot quickly replicate
```
